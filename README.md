## Selected Projects

### Automotive Aerodynamics
Wind-tunnel studies of vehicle-scale models using full-field surface diagnostics, pressure measurements, and force measurements to investigate aerodynamic loading and flow structure.

### Global Luminescent Oil Film
Application of GLOF techniques for quantitative surface pressure and skin-friction measurements on complex aerodynamic geometries.

### Delta-Wing Aerodynamics
Experimental investigation of pressure distribution and aerodynamic loading across multiple angles of attack, combining optical measurements, pressure taps, and load-cell validation.

### Topographic Hill Flow
Surface-flow investigation of three-dimensional separated flow over a topographic hill, including skin-friction, pressure, and derived surface-flow quantities.

### Oil-Film Interferometry
Development of interferometric methods for quantitative wall-shear and skin-friction measurement.

### Impinging-Jet Heat Transfer
Experimental investigation of obliquely impinging jets using temperature-sensitive paint and quantitative heat-transfer analysis.
